This site shows polygenic scores for genomes from the 1000 Genomes Project. For each PGS, you can simulate the effect of the top [n] edits on these scores. The highest impact edits are chosen for each individual.

Note that the edits simulated here are not necessarily causal. They are of variants statistically associated with traits in GWAS, which may be spurious or due to correlation with true causal variants.

Polygenic scores are estimates of an individual's genetic predisposition, not direct measures of phenotypes. Their predictive power (the portion of phenotypic variance they explain) depends on the specifics of the PGS. Follow the link to the source to learn more about the specific score. Scores assume additivity (it's a pretty good assumption for many traits), and that variant effects are linear (having a single copy of the variant is half as strong as two copies).
